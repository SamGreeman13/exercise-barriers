About this project

This research explores the common barriers that prevent people from going to the gym, drawing on qualitative interviews with 8 participants aged 18–30 in the Greater Boston area. We examined psychological barriers like body image and gym intimidation alongside logistical barriers like cost, time, and proximity.

What we found surprised us: the barriers people feel most strongly — body image, social media's influence on self-perception — are rarely the ones they name as their biggest obstacle. Instead, time and cost dominate the conversation, while deeper psychological barriers operate quietly underneath. The overlap between these two categories is where the most interesting insights live.
