What are METs?

METs (Metabolic Equivalent of Task) is a way to quantify how much energy is needed to complete a task. It is based on a baseline of 1 MET being equivalent to the energy cost of a person at rest. MET-hours is a measure of total energy volume for any activity.

Typically, any activity under 3.0 METs is considered light; anything between 3.0 and 6.0 METs is considered moderate, and anything above 6.0 METs is considered vigorous.

For adults, the average daily energy expenditure is about 40.8 MET-hours (ranging from 33.6 to 57.6). The American Heart Association recommends 2.5 hours of moderate activity (3–6 METs) or 1.25 hours of vigorous activity (>6 METs) per week.

How to set your budget

33–37 MET-h: Sedentary lifestyle — desk job, minimal movement, mostly sitting or driving.

38–43 MET-h: Lightly active — office worker who walks to lunch, does light errands. Most adults fall here.

44–50 MET-h: Moderately active — on your feet for work (retail, teaching), regular exercise.

51–58 MET-h: Very active — construction, nursing, athletics, or multiple workouts per day.

Our Interview Insights

Across all interviews, participants expressed overwhelmingly negative feelings toward social media, despite spending an average of 2–3 hours on it each day at almost no energy cost (approximately 1.15 METs). Many described the experience as addictive and emotionally draining, and several noted the frustration of investing so much time and attention into content they recognized as meaningless.

In contrast, their experiences with physical activity revealed a very different pattern. Participants who exercised regularly reported feeling their best after a workout, and they emphasized the lasting improvements in mood, confidence, and overall well‑being. Although exercise requires significantly more energy at roughly 5.5 METs for 45 minutes, it was consistently described as one of the only physically demanding parts of the day that reliably provided a positive return.

Téa Adams · Sam Greeman · Northeastern University
